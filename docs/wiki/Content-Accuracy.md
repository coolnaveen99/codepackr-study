# Content and Accuracy

Educational content should be clear enough for a student to study independently.

## Content standard

- Define the concept in simple language.
- Show the rule or formula.
- Give a worked example.
- Explain common mistakes.
- Keep terminology consistent.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Keep generated examples deterministic where calculations are involved.

When a topic is updated, review related tools and pages for consistency rather than creating duplicate explanations.
